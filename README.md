## 🔭 Currently Building: Aspect Niche

**A hobby discovery app for iPhone and iPad** that turns "I should try that" into an actual plan. Pick what you're curious about and a living, Obsidian-style graph of hobbies grows around it. Each hobby comes with a plan for your first session, places near you picked for a first visit, and a link to invite friends. Flip the graph and it becomes a record of what you've done, with your own photos and notes.

[![Aspect Niche walkthrough](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/devauntea/aspect-niche-web/main/.github/media/aspect-niche-preview.gif)](https://github.com/devauntea/aspect-niche-web/releases/download/launch-video/aspect-niche-walkthrough.mp4)

▶️ **[Watch the two-minute walkthrough](https://github.com/devauntea/aspect-niche-web/releases/download/launch-video/aspect-niche-walkthrough.mp4)** · 🌐 **[aspectniche.com](https://aspectniche.com)**

- 📱 **React Native + Expo + TypeScript** app, offline-first, with an optional account that syncs between devices
- 🗺️ **A beginner-first place ranking** over a database of over a million US places I built from Overture Maps, ranked for a first visit rather than for popularity
- 🗄️ **Supabase** (Postgres, row-level security, edge functions) with my own 68-attack security test suite
- 🧠 **AI help** that knows where you are with a hobby, plus invitations anyone can answer from a link, no app needed
- 🎨 **Eight themes, six icon sets and six node styles**, so the map looks the way you want

*The goal: help people break out of their routine and find their next obsession.*
